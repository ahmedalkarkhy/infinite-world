REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

Compliance is not a cost. It is the condition of sale.

For a platform running biometrics, computer vision and workforce management inside the European single market, regulatory proximity is not convenience. It is what separates sellable technology from unsellable technology. The European hub in Brussels is the company's compliance centre, at the institutional heart of the Union where the framework is written and interpreted.

01

GDPR

Biometric data used for unique identification is a special category. It requires a lawful basis, an impact assessment, a retention policy and a non-biometric alternative.

02

AI Act

Biometric identification and workforce management are high risk categories under Annex III of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.

03

Digital Omnibus

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force since 27 July 2026, moved the Annex III high risk obligations to 2 December 2027 and Annex I to 2 August 2028.
