THE SYSTEM IN SCREENS

Not a concept. A system in operation.

These are the real views management and teams use every day.

COMMAND

The whole operation in real time.

Active sites, check-ins, open alerts and the week's reports on a single screen, with the map of the sites on the ground, progress and occupancy included.

SITES

Progress reported against hours consumed.

The comparison that tells the truth. If the site is at 30 per cent and the hours are already at 45, there is still time to correct it.

PRESENCE

Only whoever is really there counts.

Clock-in with geolocation. The perimeter only allows a punch inside the site, photography is mandatory, and time, place and author are stored together.

SAFETY

A call for help with address and time.

The worker holds the emergency button and the alert reaches management with location and status, open or resolved, always followed through.

TEAM

The entire workforce, organised.

Every person with their role, the site they are on and the state of their contract. Directory, org chart and a centralised internal inbox.

AUDIT

Nothing is erased. Everything is proven.

An immutable trail: every operation with author, date, time, origin and version. The record only ever adds, nobody rewrites the past.

WORKER APP

The app of whoever builds.

Sign in, clock in, choose the type of punch. Voice report transcribed automatically. Notifications, payments and the emergency alert.

REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

Compliance is not a cost. It is the condition of sale.

For a platform running biometrics, computer vision and workforce management inside the European single market, regulatory proximity is not convenience. It is what separates sellable technology from unsellable technology. The European hub in Brussels is the company's compliance centre, at the institutional heart of the Union where the framework is written and interpreted.

01

GDPR

Biometric data used for unique identification is a special category. It requires a lawful basis, an impact assessment, a retention policy and a non-biometric alternative.

02

AI Act

Biometric identification and workforce management are high risk categories under Annex III of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.

03

Digital Omnibus

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force since 27 July 2026, moved the Annex III high risk obligations to 2 December 2027 and Annex I to 2 August 2028.
